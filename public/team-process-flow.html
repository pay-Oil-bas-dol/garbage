Slide 3 — Research & Suggestions

Nikhil — Domain Research & Civic Architecture

Swachh Bharat Guidelines, 4 Waste Categories, 20–50 Green Points & Brand Rewards Model

🔬
💬 Prompts & Requirements Led by Nikhil:
"Build a full-stack social environmental web application optimized for mobile browsers, designed to encourage and gamify street cleaning in India. Users can report garbage, clean it, and earn points (20-50) based on AI verification of 'Before' and 'After' photos... Categories: Plastic Waste, Organic/Wet Waste, Mixed Litter, E-Waste."
1. Waste Taxonomy

4 Municipal Categories

Aligned categories with Indian Municipal Solid Waste bylaws: Organic, Recyclable Plastic, Dangerous E-Waste, and Mixed Street Litter.

2. Economic Allotment

20–50 Green Points

Formulated point scaling based on volume, segregation accuracy, and hazardous handling (+20 for light mixed, up to +50 for segregated plastic).

3. Reward Partnerships

14 Partner Brand Alignments

Researched popular Indian merchant vouchers (Amazon Pay, Flipkart, Zomato, Swiggy, Uber, Ola) to convert green points into tangible utility.

📦 Code Outputs: src/types/index.ts (WasteCategory) & src/lib/store.ts Research Complete
Slide 4 — App Development (Core Engineering)

Divyanshu & Aritra — Full-Stack App Engineering

Next.js 16, React 19, Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision AI & Supabase Database

💻
💬 Prompts & User Requests Executed:
"Frontend: Next.js (App Router, React), Tailwind CSS... Backend/Database: Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Edge Storage)... AI Integration: Google Gemini API (Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision) for image analysis... Dedicated visually engaging loading screen: 'Verifying and allotting points...' with animated scanning radar."
1. Mobile Viewport UI

Outdoor High-Contrast Shell

Engineered `max-w-md` mobile container with bottom thumb navigation, smooth tab switching, tactile eco-cards, and daylight-readable green styling.

2. Vision AI Pipeline

Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision Route

Coded `/api/verify` serverless route: parses base64 Before/After buffers, enforces structured JSON schema, and verifies landmark consistency.

3. Data & Resilience

Supabase + Local Reactive Store

Wrote PostgreSQL schema (`missions`, `profiles`, `rewards`) with RLS policies and built-in offline localStorage fallback for zero-fail demonstrations.

📦 Code Outputs: src/app/page.tsx, /api/verify/route.ts, MissionWizard.tsx Built & Verified

Anurag & Sobhit — UX QA & AI Anti-Fraud Validation

Projector Readability Feedback, Light Green Theme Suggestion & Fake Meme Verification
